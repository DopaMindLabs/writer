# Notebooks

Notebooks keep photographed or imported pages together inside a space. They live under
**Workshop**, separately from Brain Space notes and ordinary documents.

## Create a notebook

Open the **Workshop** menu in the sidebar and choose **New notebook**. You can keep
several notebooks in one space. Use a notebook's menu to rename it or delete it and all
of its pages.

## Add pages

Use **Choose photos** to add one or several images. On a phone or tablet, **Take photo**
asks for the rear camera when the browser supports it. Imported images are prepared
before they are saved; the original upload is not kept as a second hidden copy.

Each page appears in the thumbnail rail. Select a thumbnail to open the page, or use the
previous and next controls. The selected page is part of the URL, so browser Back and
Forward follow page changes.

## Organise pages

Open a page's menu to rotate it, move it earlier or later, or delete it. Deleting the
last page returns focus to the add-page action so you can continue with the keyboard.

On smaller screens the thumbnail rail runs across the top instead of down the side.

## Your data

Notebook pages are part of their space. Space backups and archive restore/import include
the notebooks, their page order and their saved page assets.
