# Working in multiple tabs

Open the same document in more than one browser tab and your edits stay in
sync. Type in one tab and the words appear in the other straight away — there's
nothing to switch on and no button to press.

## At a glance

- **Live sync** — every tab on this device shares the same document. An edit in
  one shows up in the others as you type.
- **Merged automatically** — if two tabs change the document at the same time,
  both sets of changes are kept and merged; nothing is overwritten.
- **Your cursor is shared** — each tab shows the others' cursors, labelled with
  your display name and colour from [Your account](your-account).
- **Still local** — collaboration happens entirely in your browser. Your writing
  never leaves your device unless you export it — see [Your data](your-data).

## How it works

Each document has a single shared history. When you type, the change is saved
locally and passed to every other tab viewing the same document, which applies
it in place. Because the tabs merge changes rather than replacing whole
documents, editing the same paragraph from two tabs at once is safe — the merge
keeps both edits.

## Undo and redo

Undo and redo are **yours**. Undoing in one tab only steps back through the
edits you made in that tab — it won't reach in and undo a change made in
another tab.

## Presence

When another tab has its cursor in the document, you'll see a small labelled
caret where that cursor sits, in the colour set for your account. Change your
name or colour under **Settings → Account** — see
[Your account](your-account).

## Restoring a version

Restoring an earlier version updates the document live in every open tab. The
restore flows through the editor just like a normal edit, so the other tabs
converge on the restored text and a **pre-restore** snapshot is saved first, in
case you change your mind. See [Version history](version-history).
