# Your profile

Your **profile** sets the name and colour shown with your cursor when the same
document is open in multiple tabs. Open it from **Settings → Profile**.

## At a glance

- **Display name** — the name beside your cursor in another tab. Leave it blank
  to appear as *Anonymous*.
- **Presence colour** — the hue that will mark your cursor and text selection to
  other tabs.
- **On-device only** — your profile never leaves this browser.

## Display name

Type the name to show beside your cursor. Leave it empty to appear as
*Anonymous*. The name is saved as you edit.

## Presence colour

Pick the colour that marks your cursor and selection in other tabs, from five muted
hues: **terracotta**, **slate blue**, **moss**, **plum**, or **ochre**. The chosen
swatch is ringed.

The swatches form a single control. Click one to choose it, or move between them
from the keyboard: **Left**/**Up** and **Right**/**Down** step through the hues
(wrapping around at the ends), and **Home**/**End** jump to the first or last.

## Your privacy

Your profile is saved locally in this browser and used by its open Writer tabs.
Clearing your browser data removes it along with the rest of your local data — see
[Your data](your-data) for storage and backup options.
